Our Story

Where Warmth Meets
Refined Elegance

Marks the Day Events was born from a simple belief: the most beautiful weddings feel like an extension of the people getting married — unhurried, deeply personal, and filled with the kind of details that guests talk about for years.

Based in the heart of Tennessee, we bring the warmth of Southern hospitality together with a refined eye for design. Whether you're envisioning a golden-hour barn reception in Franklin or a sweeping mountain ceremony in the Smokies, we are here to make every moment unforgettable.
